tend privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: your data stays on your phone. tend has no ads and no tracking, and needs no account. A few things can leave the phone, each only if you choose it: anonymous usage counts, your town (for the camp's weather), and, if you log in, your account, a backup of your camp and what friends see.

What tend uses, and why

  • Usage access (which app is open, and for how long): to show the pause when you open an app you picked and to count your minutes.
  • Display over other apps: to show the pause screen over that app.
  • Health Connect (only if you connect it): steps, exercise sessions and sleep, read to complete the matching activities and give you minutes back. tend only reads; it never writes or shares health data.
  • Notifications (only if you allow them): the morning plan, a heads-up when minutes run low, the evening recap and the Sunday letter.
  • The apps installed on your phone: only to let you pick which ones to pause.

Where your data is

Only on your phone (apart from the optional counts above), in tend's private storage: your settings, daily minutes, finished activities, your camp and acorns, and your answers to "Worth it?" and "What are you looking for?". tend does not send any of this off the device, does not use it for advertising and does not sell or share it.

Anonymous usage stats (optional, off until you say yes)

tend asks once whether you'd like to share anonymous counts that help make it better. If you tap yes, it sends small events such as "the app was opened", "a pause ended in Not now", "a walk was done" or "yesterday: 40 minutes used, 20 left". Each comes with a random id made on your phone (not linked to you, your account or your device), the day since you installed tend, the app version and the Android version.

It never sends your name, contacts, which apps you use, health data or what you typed. Until you answer, these counts stay on your phone; if you say no, they are deleted and nothing is sent. You can turn it off any time in Settings, and Start over gives you a new random id. The counts are stored with Supabase (our database host), used only to improve tend, and never sold or used for ads. Sent over HTTPS.

Your account (optional)

tend works fully without an account. If you log in (with Google, or with a 6-digit code sent to your e-mail), we keep:

  • Your name and e-mail address: to log you in. With Google we get your name and e-mail from Google; we never see your Google password.
  • Your profile: the name you use in tend, your companion (Bo or Mia) and your level. Friends you add see these and your camp; nobody else does.
  • Friends: who your friends are, your invite code, and what you do to each other's camps (a wave, a visit, toilet paper on the oak, a cheer). Notes are deleted after 30 days; removing a friend or deleting your account removes what's shared.
  • Your town (optional, typed by you): its name is sent to Open-Meteo, a free weather service, to find it, and its rounded coordinates (about a kilometre) to get the weather for the camp, about once an hour. tend never asks for or uses your phone's location. Open-Meteo gets no account or id from tend.
  • Age range and gender (optional): kept on your phone (and in your backup, if you log in). If you share anonymous usage counts, they go with them as ranges, together with your town's country (never the town), to see who tend helps. Friends never see them.
  • For friends to see: how your camp is arranged, when you're doing a phone-free activity and until when (so your companion can sit at their campfire), and what you did last, in a few words like "went for a walk" (your own activities show only as "something of their own"). The last activity is shown for two days.
  • A backup: your settings, your camp and acorns, which activities you did on which day, minutes used per day and your "Worth it?" answers, so you can restore them on a new phone. It is saved when you log in, about twice a day, and when you tap "Back up now".

The backup never contains your health readings (steps, exercise, sleep) or which other apps you use. Account data is stored with Supabase (our database host, servers in Ireland, EU), sent over HTTPS, used only to run tend, and never sold, shared or used for ads.

Deleting it: Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account, profile and backup from the server right away. You can also e-mail botondjani@gmail.com and we'll delete it for you. Logging out or uninstalling leaves the backup on the server until you delete the account.

Sharing on purpose

If you tap "Export test data" or "Share crash report", tend opens your phone's share sheet with that text, and you choose where it goes. Nothing is sent without that tap.

Deleting your data

Uninstall tend, or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Everything is gone; there's no copy anywhere else. Health Connect access can be removed any time in Health Connect's settings.

Children

tend is not designed for children under 13.
