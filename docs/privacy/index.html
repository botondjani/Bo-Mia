tend privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

In short: everything tend knows stays on your phone. tend has no account, no ads and no tracking. The only thing that can leave the phone is anonymous usage counts, and only if you say yes.

What tend uses, and why

  • Usage access (which app is open, and for how long): to show the pause when you open an app you picked and to count your minutes.
  • Display over other apps: to show the pause screen over that app.
  • Health Connect (only if you connect it): steps, exercise sessions and sleep, read to complete the matching activities and give you minutes back. tend only reads; it never writes or shares health data.
  • Notifications (only if you allow them): the morning plan, a heads-up when minutes run low, the evening recap and the Sunday letter.
  • The apps installed on your phone: only to let you pick which ones to pause.

Where your data is

Only on your phone (apart from the optional counts above), in tend's private storage: your settings, daily minutes, finished activities, your camp and acorns, and your answers to "Worth it?" and "What are you looking for?". tend does not send any of this off the device, does not use it for advertising and does not sell or share it.

Anonymous usage stats (optional, off until you say yes)

tend asks once whether you'd like to share anonymous counts that help make it better. If you tap yes, it sends small events such as "the app was opened", "a pause ended in Not now", "a walk was done" or "yesterday: 40 minutes used, 20 left". Each comes with a random id made on your phone (not linked to you, your account or your device), the day since you installed tend, the app version and the Android version.

It never sends your name, contacts, which apps you use, health data or what you typed. Until you answer, these counts stay on your phone; if you say no, they are deleted and nothing is sent. You can turn it off any time in Settings, and Start over gives you a new random id. The counts are stored with Supabase (our database host), used only to improve tend, and never sold or used for ads. Sent over HTTPS.

Sharing on purpose

If you tap "Export test data" or "Share crash report", tend opens your phone's share sheet with that text, and you choose where it goes. Nothing is sent without that tap.

Deleting your data

Uninstall tend, or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Everything is gone; there's no copy anywhere else. Health Connect access can be removed any time in Health Connect's settings.

Children

tend is not designed for children under 13.
