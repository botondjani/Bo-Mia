# tend

*Take care of you. Offline.* A screen-time companion for Android: you get a small free budget each day and earn the rest by doing real things. Bo or Mia, a small blob character, reflects how your day is going.

This is the MVP skeleton described in the concept doc. Native Android, English only, distributed as a side-loaded APK.

## What works

| Part | Where |
|---|---|
| Budget logic: free base, earned minutes that expire each day, manual-credit cap, lock at ≤ 0, delay before every open (5 s, +1 s each time, at most 10 s), unused minutes go to the garden | `core/Budget.kt` (unit-tested) |
| Activities the user picks (up to 6, nothing pre-selected): workout (30 min exercise), walk (4,000 steps) and sleep (7 h) via Health Connect; read (20 min) and learn (30 min) via a focus timer; friends & family, hobby (30 min), tidy up and cooking (20 min) as phone-free sessions (screen on at most 3 min, measured from the system's screen events, finishing in the background with a notification); own activities by check-off with undo (daily cap 45 min) | `core/Tasks.kt`, `ui/ActivityPicker.kt`, `health/` |
| Health Connect: reads today's steps + exercise minutes (read-only) on app open, every minute while open, and before every pause screen; progress bars and a celebration when a task completes | `health/HealthConnect.kt`, `health/TaskSync.kt` |
| Moods (happy / tired / scrolling / outside); the companion line looks back at what we did today ("Today we already went outside and learned something. Look at us!"); finished tasks sink to the bottom | `core/Mood.kt`, `core/Tasks.kt` |
| Monitoring: UsageStats polled every 0.3 s, opens a delay screen, counts time used, locks at zero, logs every miss | `monitor/MonitorService.kt` |
| Pause pop-up: a card in the middle of the screen, shown in the first frame (sync runs behind it), the app blurred behind (Android 12+; when the system turns blur off, a darker scrim keeps the look), a "We tend to…" line, "What are you looking for?" chips; the main button is "Do this instead" (the next unfinished activity: starts its timer, or a walk / move / bed suggestion), then "Not now", with "Open" as a countdown link. Every pause logs its outcome (instead / not now / open / earn / left) | `monitor/InterstitialActivity.kt` |
| Evening mode: from 21, 22 or 23:00 (until 5:00) the pause waits at least 15 s and the companion is sleepy ("It's late…"); on by default from 22:00, toggle in settings | `core/Budget.kt`, `monitor/InterstitialActivity.kt` |
| Sunday letter: Sunday 18:30 notification ("You got back X and N plants grew. You let the scroll go K of M times."), tap opens the garden & week page; toggle in settings | `reminder/WeeklyLetter.kt` |
| "Worth it?" pop-up over the home screen, three emoji tiles, after sessions of 30 s or more | `monitor/WorthItActivity.kt` |
| Evening recap (from 19:00): minutes got back, tonight's plant, steps / exercise / reading, "Worth it?" summary with late-night pattern, home milestones | `core/Garden.kt`, `ui/Garden.kt` |
| Garden: one plant per day from unused minutes (sprout < 15, tulip < 30, sunflower ≥ 30), planted at midnight; the home grows weekly (lit window + bench in week 2, own tree in week 4) | `core/Garden.kt`, `ui/Garden.kt` |
| Garden & week page (no tab bar; reached from the "Garden" pill next to today's minutes, system back returns home): garden scene, things, tonight's plant, how it grows, "Your home grows with you" (one track with week 1/2/4 and what comes next), then the last 7 days: screen time per day, minutes got back, "The pause" (how often you let the scroll go), "Worth it?" by time of day and by app, looking-for answers, one insight sentence | `ui/Garden.kt`, `core/Patterns.kt`, `ui/Patterns.kt` |
| Evening reminder: offered once on the recap, toggle + 20/21/22:00 in settings; inexact daily alarm, silent if the recap was already opened, rescheduled after reboot | `reminder/RecapReminder.kt` |
| Home screen widgets: 1×1 (the companion), 2×1 (face, minutes left or what earns them back, "Bo is happy"), 2×2 (the garden with minutes left); redrawn only when something visible changes; the widget picker shows a real preview (sample Bo and garden) | `widget/TendWidget.kt` |
| Onboarding (8 steps incl. "What would you rather do?", suggested apps, in-context permissions), settings, home, focus timer | `ui/` |
| Accessories from a week of habits: sweatband (4 workout days), reading glasses (4 reading/learning days), sun hat (4 days outside), flower (3 days with friends or a hobby); faded ones wait "on the shelf" on the garden page | `core/Accessories.kt` |
| Character: Rive rig `assets/tend_character.riv`, generated by `tools/rive/make_character.py` (moods incl. reading, Bo/Mia, accessories, breathing, blinking, glancing, tap hop; see `docs/rive-rig.md`); drawn fallback for the widget | `ui/Character.kt`, `tools/rive/` |
| Brand: Moss/Mint/Coral/Sunny/Sky/Ink/Sage, Fredoka + Nunito (OFL, bundled); design tokens and shared components | `ui/Theme.kt`, `ui/Components.kt` |
| Icons: tend's own line icon set (24 vector drawables, 24 grid, 1.8 stroke, round caps) in tinted circles, no emoji | `res/drawable/ic_t_*.xml`, `ui/TendIcons.kt` |

Logs are written to the app's private storage: `answers.csv` (looking-for and worth-it answers), `history.csv` (one line per closed day) and `misses.csv` (blocking gaps or opens that weren't caught). Settings → **Export test data** shares all of them (plus the last crash and device info) as one text.

## Not done yet

- **Background Health Connect reads**: data is read only while tend is on screen (home or pause screen). The widget can lag until then.
- **Step source**: Health Connect needs an app that writes steps (Google Fit, Samsung Health, Fitbit, or the phone's built-in step tracking on newer Pixels). Workouts count once a tracker writes an exercise session.
- Out of MVP scope (from the concept doc): floating character, friends, coins/shop, iOS.

## Build

Requires JDK 17+ and the Android SDK (platform 36). The APK contains ARM native code only (Rive), so it runs on phones but not on x86 emulators.

```bash
./gradlew testDebugUnitTest   # domain logic tests
./gradlew assembleRelease     # app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release.apk (signed with the debug key, for side-loading)
```

## Test setup on the phone

Install the APK and follow the onboarding: welcome, companion, how it works, apps (common time sinks are pre-selected), budget, then two switches.

- **Display over other apps** comes first. Once it's on, Android lets tend bring itself back to the front, so after the second switch the user returns to the app automatically.
- **Usage access** comes second.
- **Unlock first**: Android 13+ locks these switches for side-loaded apps ("restricted settings"). For any install not from an app store, onboarding starts with: App info → ⋮ → *Allow restricted settings*; coming back from App info counts as done. If the menu item is missing, a link lets Android refuse a switch once, after which it appears. Settings pages are opened scrolled to tend where the phone supports it, with a short on-screen hint. For testers using adb: `adb shell appops set app.tend ACCESS_RESTRICTED_SETTINGS allow`.
- **Health Connect** is connected from the home screen ("Connect Health Connect"). On Android 13 and below the button opens the Play Store to install it first.
- The reading timer keeps running when you leave tend. Opening a paused app from the pause screen ends it.
- **Limited mode**: "Continue without" skips the switches after explaining what stops working. Without usage access tend can't measure screen time (activities, timer and garden still work). Without the overlay there's no pause pop-up; minutes are still counted and a notification nudges instead (notification permission is asked for that). The home screen shows what's missing.
- **Widget**: not part of onboarding. From the second day, a one-time home card ("Bo has a widget") opens a three-step guide with "Go to home screen". Settings has the button too; once placed, it shows a confirmation.
- Notification permission is otherwise asked only when you turn on the evening reminder. Until then the monitor's notification stays hidden.
- **Crash reports**: after a crash, the home screen offers "Share crash report" (stack trace, or a note when the Rive character's native code stopped the app). If Rive ever takes the app down, it switches itself off and the drawn character is used until a new APK is installed.
- On Xiaomi, Samsung, Huawei and similar phones, turn off battery optimisation for tend, or the monitor gets killed (this shows up in the miss log).
